Approved visual system

Quiet mobility.

A calm, light-first interface for finding a way home. Sage creates trust, carbon creates clarity, and gently layered surfaces add depth without clutter.

Why this wins

Premium without looking precious.

Zoox restraint, Transit hierarchy, and Apple-like spatial layering. Curves follow movement; highlights and soft shadows clarify which surface sits above another.

Color system

Six colors. One job each.

No gradients, neon glows, or decorative accent colors. Amber and red appear only when the trip state genuinely needs them.

01 · TYPE
Instrument Sans

Variable width and weight create hierarchy without mixing families or looking generic.

02 · SHAPE
Curves imply motion

Continuous corners, curved routes, and soft sheet edges echo movement through campus.

03 · DEPTH
Layered, not shiny

Three clear planes: map, floating controls, and trip sheet. Subtle highlights explain depth.

04 · COLOR
Action earns color

Mint means “continue.” It never decorates headings, icons, cards, or empty space.

First-screen proof

Depth you can understand.

The map is the ground plane. Controls float above it. The trip sheet rises closest to the user. Every curved edge and shadow explains the interface instead of decorating it.

Hierarchy

Destination and action first; settings and map tools recede.

Signature

Curved route geometry and raised Mint nodes make the journey feel physical.

Material

Flat tonal surfaces, controlled shadows, and no glassy visual noise.
